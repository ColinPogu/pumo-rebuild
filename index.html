Frequently asked questions

Good questions, straight answers.

How do you know what to change? +

We compare your listing with the ones competing for the same shopper, including photos, video, reviews, and price. Then we pick the changes that look worth making and rank them by impact.

What if my listing is already fine? +

Then your audit confirms it, and Radar keeps watching the set so you hear about it the moment a competitor makes a move that changes the picture.

Do I need to connect Seller Central? +

No. An ASIN or listing URL is enough to run your first audit. Connecting more data only makes the recommendations sharper.

Will my creatives look AI generated? +

They are built from your own product photos to match your listing, and you always get a brief so your photographer can shoot the same shot instead.

Do I own the images and video? +

Yes. Everything Pumo creates for your listing is yours to use, keep, and publish wherever you sell.

What if I do not have a listing yet? +

Start from a competitor you want to beat. Pumo shows you the creative and content jobs a strong listing in your category needs to cover.
